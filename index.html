Hi, fellow human! I am Ishita, the mind behind Vivid and Grey. I am an artist and a visual designer currently living a freelancer's life in coastal Goa, India.

My design work over the years has been multifaceted, ranging from contract illustration work to full-time graphic design. I've worked with clients from different industries and parts of the world. This diversity is a reflection of my pursuit of the new, seeing each design as a voyage and a chance to give my best effort. If my work looks like a good fit for your project, please email me at ishita.nager@outlook.com

Beyond professional design services, my interests lie in life, the universe, and everything. I spend my time creating art that breathes both the 'vivids' and the 'greys' of the human experience and that of nature. Through these themes of duality and depth, I craft experiences that resonate with the soul and kindle a shared sense of wonder, acceptance, and appreciation. You can choose to take home some of my artwork from the original art section or ping me for a custom order.

As a hobby, I create fantasy dreamscapes: strange, beautiful, melancholic worlds and visual stories that let you momentarily slip out of the matrix we call modern life. Each cinematic "Dream Freeze" video is carefully stitched from AI-generated visuals, perfected with ambient sound design and visual effects, crafted to evoke emotion, curiosity, or quiet awe.

Otherwise, I might also be found embracing a challenge (like developing this website from scratch!) or lost in either the sunset's hue or Claire de Lune.
