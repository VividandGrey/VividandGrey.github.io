The name Vivid and Grey reflects the personal philosophy of its creator Ishita: that life is both bright and bleak, and both sides deserve to be felt deeply. Her art explores themes of Cosmos, Consciousness, and Clarity. The first and second pertain to the big questions of life, the universe, and everything. The third pertains to transcendental awakening and interconnectedness, especially after a period of grief and confusion.

Motivated by her quest for Qualia, Ishita likes to depict these themes via a monochrome palette – another nod to the brand name. Painting in black and white oils (and often an indulgent phthalo blue), her work feels mindful and conscious.

From premium originals for seasoned collectors to accessible pieces for those just starting their home decor journey, there is a 'Vivid and Grey' artwork for every space. If you are looking for a signature piece or a custom commission to complete a vision, let’s start a conversation.

Collaborations with interior design firms seeking to integrate authentic, bespoke emotive art into their residential or commercial portfolios, are also welcome. Please reach out for trade inquiries at hi@vividandgrey.com or via Instagram.
